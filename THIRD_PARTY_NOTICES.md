# Third-party notices

The project source tree contains third-party components. Their original
license notices are retained beside the relevant source files and take
precedence for those components.

In particular, the WIZnet ioLibrary Driver is distributed with its original
license text in each firmware source folder as `LICENSE-ioLibrary.txt`.

Raspberry Pi Pico SDK and lwIP components are obtained through the normal Pico
SDK build process and remain under their respective upstream licenses.
